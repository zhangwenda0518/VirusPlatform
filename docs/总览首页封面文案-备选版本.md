## 版本 A · 科研叙事版（推荐）

**视角**：病毒组学是一门方法论，平台是它的完整实现。讲"从数据到结论"的
闭环与多证据互证，学术汇报、课题展示、对外交流都站得住。

### 中文

- **定位句**：从一份测序数据，到一整套站得住的病毒学结论。
- **副句**：面向低丰度植物 RNA 病毒组：定性、定量、定宿主、可溯源，产物直接支撑论文与 NCBI 提交。
- **工程口径**：Windows 单机零依赖 · 内置病毒数据库 3.4 GB · 13 步级联默认全程离线

- **Ⅰ 背景**：植物病毒病难治，防控的前提是看得清。宏转录组一次读出样品的完整病毒组成，正在取代单一靶标检测，成为病毒发现与鉴定的主流；但分析流程散落在服务器与十几款命令行工具之间——会测序易，得结论难。

- **Ⅱ 方法**：以多证据互证为纲：真比对与伪比对双引擎交叉识别定量已知病毒，宏基因组组装重建未知病毒基因组，ICTV 宿主级联与 2340 万公共样本溯源互相印证，三层注释为远缘 ORF 兜底——每个结论至少两条独立证据。

- **Ⅲ 流程**：一条从数据到成果的闭环：SRA/GSA 公共数据检索下载，或本地 FASTQ 直入分析；13 步级联自动衔接，终点是鉴定定量表、基因组图、进化树、引物与可提交序列——人只做判断，不做串联。

- **Ⅳ 意义**：过去要生信团队加服务器集群才能完成的病毒组学分析，如今一台 Windows 电脑即可承担；数据不出本机、断点可续、结论可复现，序列可直接提交 GenBank/BioSample——把分析能力交还实验室。

### English

- **Lead**: From one sequencing dataset to a body of viral evidence that stands up to scrutiny.
- **Sub**: Built for low-abundance plant RNA viromes: identify, quantify, assign hosts and trace provenance — outputs feed straight into papers and NCBI submission.
- **Eng**: Windows-native, zero dependencies · 3.4 GB virus databases built in · 13-stage cascade, fully offline by default
- **Background**: Plant virus diseases cannot be cured — control depends on seeing clearly, early. Metatranscriptomics reads out a sample's complete virus composition in one run and is becoming the mainstream route to virus discovery and identification; yet the analysis stack is scattered across servers and a dozen command-line tools. Sequencing is easy — conclusions are not.
- **Methods**: Built on cross-validation of independent evidence: dual true-/pseudo-alignment engines identify and quantify known viruses; metagenomic assembly rebuilds unknown genomes; the ICTV host-probability cascade and LOGAN tracing over 23.4M public samples corroborate each other; three-layer annotation rescues distant ORFs — every conclusion rests on at least two independent lines of evidence.
- **Workflow**: A closed loop from data to deliverables: retrieve public SRA/GSA data or load local FASTQ; a 13-stage cascade chains automatically, ending not in a pile of intermediate files but in quantification tables, genome maps, phylogenies, primers and submission-ready sequences — people judge, the platform chains.
- **Impact**: Virome analysis that once required a bioinformatics team and a server cluster now runs on one Windows PC; data never leaves the machine, runs resume from breakpoints, conclusions reproduce, and sequences go straight to GenBank/BioSample — analysis capability, returned to the lab.

## 版本 B · 诊断实务版

**视角**：站在植物检疫、种业健康、病害普查的一线讲"灵敏、定量、可溯源"
这套诊断学金标准，行业味最浓。

### 中文

- **定位句**：灵敏检出 · 准确定量 · 结论可溯源。
- **副句**：面向植物病毒诊断全场景：低丰度不漏检、复合侵染不漏种、候选病毒定宿主，阳性结论可回溯公共数据佐证。
- **工程口径**：单机全流程 · 默认离线 · 断点续跑 · 每项结论附产物级证据

- **Ⅰ 背景**：植物病毒病无法治愈，防控完全依赖早期精准检出。常规 PCR 与血清学一次只能回答"是否感染某一种已知病毒"，低丰度与复合侵染常被漏掉；宏转录组一次读出全部病毒组成，正成为检疫鉴定与新病毒预警的主流手段。

- **Ⅱ 方法**：按诊断学"灵敏、特异、可溯源"三重标准设计：宿主去除与双引擎比对压低检出下限与假阳性，ICTV 宿主概率级联（5.9 万条宿主记录）与 2340 万公共样本溯源为每个结论提供独立佐证。

- **Ⅲ 流程**：样品进、报告出：质控、去宿主、已知病毒识别与定量、组装验证、宿主预测、进化分析、引物设计到可视化报告自动衔接——鉴定、定量、溯源、引物，一次运行全部完成。

- **Ⅳ 意义**：检疫检测、种子健康与病害普查团队在自己的电脑上完成分子鉴定：不需要生信人员，不需要服务器，不把样品数据交给任何外部平台；检测结果连同特异性引物与进化证据，直接支撑处置决策与成果发表。

### English

- **Lead**: Sensitive detection · accurate quantification · traceable conclusions.
- **Sub**: For the full spectrum of plant virus diagnostics: low-abundance infections are not missed, mixed infections are fully resolved, candidate viruses get host calls, and positives are traceable through public data.
- **Eng**: Single-machine workflow · offline by default · resumable · every conclusion ships with evidence files
- **Background**: Plant virus diseases cannot be cured; control rests entirely on early, accurate detection. PCR and serology answer one question at a time — infected with this known virus or not — while low-abundance or mixed infections slip through. Metatranscriptomics reads the whole virus composition at once and is becoming the mainstream tool for quarantine identification and new-virus warning.
- **Methods**: Designed to diagnostic standards — sensitive, specific, traceable: host removal and dual-engine alignment lower detection limits and false positives; the ICTV host-probability cascade (59,000 host records) and LOGAN tracing over 23.4M public samples independently corroborate every conclusion.
- **Workflow**: Samples in, report out: QC, host removal, known-virus identification and quantification, assembly and verification, host prediction, phylogenetics, primer design and a visual report chain automatically — identification, quantification, tracing and primers in a single run.
- **Impact**: Quarantine, seed-health and survey teams run molecular identification on their own computers: no bioinformatician, no server, and no sample data ever handed to an external platform — results arrive with species-specific primers and phylogenetic evidence, ready for decisions and publication.

## 版本 C · 一体化平台版

**视角**：工程/产品视角，讲"把 pipeline 变成 product"——业内少有的
"检索 → 分析 → 溯源 → 提交"全链路闭环与低门槛，适合推广演示。

### 中文

- **定位句**：从公共数据检索到 NCBI 提交，全链路一站完成。
- **副句**：数据检索下载、13 步级联分析、公共样本溯源、提交文件生成，尽在一个图形界面；鼠标点击替代命令行，普通电脑替代服务器。
- **工程口径**：Windows 原生零依赖 · 超低内存引擎 · 中英双语 · 默认全程离线

- **Ⅰ 背景**：测序成本一路下探，分析能力却成为新瓶颈：主流病毒组流程依赖 Linux 与十余款工具的手工拼接，格式转换、参数调优、结果整合全靠人力，换个机器、换个版本结果就难复现——"拿到数据"与"产出结论"之间隔着一道专业鸿沟。

- **Ⅱ 方法**：用工程化手段重构全链条：超低内存 k-mer 引擎让普通 PC 承载基因组级宿主去除；双引擎定量、双模式组装、三层注释、双支持值建树，每个环节取最稳的算法组合；阶段产物契约化衔接，断点续跑、缺件自动降级、参数全程留痕。

- **Ⅲ 流程**：13 步级联逐级解锁，每步自动吃进上一步产物；实时进度与剩余时间预估，结果在线预览，报告一键生成；全流程只在需要决策处等待人，其余交给机器。

- **Ⅳ 意义**：当分析门槛降到双击启动，病毒组学就从少数团队的专业技能变成公共能力；默认离线让敏感数据留在本机，步步留痕让每个结论经得起复现检验——一台电脑，撑起一间分析实验室。

### English

- **Lead**: From public data search to NCBI submission — the whole chain, one workstation.
- **Sub**: Data retrieval and download, a 13-stage cascade, public-sample tracing and submission-file generation, all in one graphical interface; mouse clicks instead of command lines, an ordinary PC instead of a server.
- **Eng**: Windows-native, zero dependencies · ultra-low-memory engine · bilingual · fully offline by default
- **Background**: Sequencing keeps getting cheaper while analysis becomes the new bottleneck: mainstream virome pipelines depend on Linux and a dozen hand-stitched tools; format conversion, parameter tuning and result integration are all manual, and results stop reproducing the moment machines or versions change — between "having data" and "delivering conclusions" sits a professional moat.
- **Methods**: Rebuild the chain through engineering: an ultra-low-memory k-mer engine lets an ordinary PC carry genome-scale host removal; dual-engine quantification, dual-mode assembly, three-layer annotation and dual-support phylogenetics pick the most robust algorithm combination at each step; stages connect through output contracts, with resumable runs, automatic degradation for missing tools and full parameter provenance.
- **Workflow**: 13 stages unlock in sequence, each consuming the previous stage's outputs; live progress with time-remaining estimates, in-browser result previews, one-click report regeneration; the pipeline waits for human judgment only where judgment is needed.
- **Impact**: When the barrier drops to double-clicking an icon, virome analysis stops being a specialist skill and becomes a shared capability; offline-by-default keeps sensitive data local, and per-stage provenance makes every conclusion survive a re-run — one computer can carry an analysis lab.

## 版本 D · 科学发现版

**视角**：最有感染力的"发现"叙事，把低丰度 RNA 病毒这个定位推到台前，
宣传语感最强；四卡写法从"候选 → 确证"的科学过程切入。

### 中文

- **定位句**：看见低丰度样本里的病毒世界。
- **副句**：把低丰度 RNA 病毒从海量宿主 reads 中找出来、拼成基因组、注上功能、排进进化树，并回答它们还出现在哪些公共样本里。
- **工程口径**：单机本地分析 · 内置病毒数据库 3.4 GB · 默认全程离线

- **Ⅰ 背景**：宏转录组已成为植物病毒发现的主流手段，但"读到一条序列"与"确认一种病毒"之间隔着十几个环节：基因组重建、功能注释、宿主判定、进化定位、公共数据佐证——缺任何一环，候选都只是候选。

- **Ⅱ 方法**：发现与验证双轨并行：比对引擎对已知病毒灵敏检出并定量，组装引擎为未知病毒重建基因组；三层注释给 ORF 定功能，ICTV 级联预测宿主，最终以 2340 万公共样本溯源收尾——新记录的每个属性都有证据链。

- **Ⅲ 流程**：原始 reads 进，基因组图谱出：质控、去宿主、识别、组装、验证、注释、建树、引物、基因组图、报告，13 步自动衔接——一条候选病毒的"全套尽调"，一次运行完成。

- **Ⅳ 意义**：一个团队能做出多少病毒发现，取决于分析链条的完备程度。平台把从检出到发表的全部环节集成进一台 Windows 电脑——让发现不再取决于谁有服务器与生信团队，而取决于谁有好样品、好问题。

### English

- **Lead**: See the virus world hiding in low-abundance samples.
- **Sub**: Lift low-abundance RNA viruses out of a flood of host reads, assemble them into genomes, annotate their genes, place them on the tree — and answer where else they appear across public samples.
- **Eng**: Local single-machine analysis · 3.4 GB virus databases built in · fully offline by default
- **Background**: Metatranscriptomics is now the mainstream route to plant virus discovery, yet between "reading a sequence" and "confirming a virus" lie a dozen steps — genome reconstruction, functional annotation, host assignment, phylogenetic placement, public-data corroboration. Miss any one, and a candidate stays a candidate.
- **Methods**: Discovery and verification run in parallel: alignment engines sensitively detect and quantify known viruses while assembly rebuilds unknown genomes; three-layer annotation assigns ORF functions, the ICTV cascade predicts hosts, and tracing across 23.4M public samples closes the case — every attribute of a new record has an evidence chain.
- **Workflow**: Raw reads in, genome maps out: QC, host removal, identification, assembly, verification, annotation, phylogeny, primers, genome plots and report — 13 stages chained automatically; a candidate virus's full due diligence in one run.
- **Impact**: How much discovery a team can extract from sequencing depends on how complete its analysis chain is. The platform integrates every step from detection to publication on one Windows computer — so discovery depends no longer on who owns servers and bioinformaticians, but on who has the best samples and questions.

## 怎么选 / 怎么改

- 四版数字口径均来自 README 实测值：2340 万（LOGAN 公共样本）、5.9 万条
  （ICTV 宿主概率表）、3.4 GB（内置数据库）、13 步级联、双引擎/双模式/
  三层注释/双支持值，无虚构数据。
- 可整版采用，也可混搭（例：A 的四卡 + D 的定位句；B 的"意义"卡拼进 A）。
- 选定后需要改动的文件只有两个：`webapp/templates/home.html`（无需动，
  键名不变）与 `webapp/static/i18n.js`（替换 `hm.heroLead / hm.heroSub /
  hm.heroEng / hm.cover.*` 中英两组值）。
- 注意：四卡比现版各长约一行（约 2 行 → 3 行），`.cover-grid` 四列布局
  可正常容纳；若嫌高可砍各卡最后一句。
